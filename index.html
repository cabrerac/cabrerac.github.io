Christian Cabrera-Jojoa

My name is Christian Cabrera-Jojoa. I am an Assistant Research Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at the University of Cambridge, an Academic Associate at Pembroke College, and an affiliated researcher at Karolinska Institutet. CV.

I lead the Interfaces research programme at ML@CL. My research combines data-oriented architectures, self-sustaining multi-agent systems, and interpretability, exploring how to design, develop, deploy, and decommission AI-based systems while keeping humans in control.

Previously, I was a Research Fellow at Trinity College Dublin, where I also received a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2020). My research at Trinity focused on context-aware, pervasive, and resilient systems architectures for large, distributed, and dynamic urban environments.
