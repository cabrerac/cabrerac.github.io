Christian Cabrera-Jojoa is an Assistant Research Professor and Affiliated Lecturer at the Department of Computer Science and Technology, and an Academic Associate at Pembroke College, at the University of Cambridge, UK.

My current research addresses the problems and challenges that arise from the deployment of Artificial Intelligence Systems in the real world. Particularly, I am developing approaches for designing self-sustaining AI-based systems to improve their interpretability and autonomous adaptation while keeping them under human control and steering.

Previously, I was a Research Fellow at Trinity College Dublin, where I also received a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2020). My research at Trinity focused on context-aware, pervasive, and resilient systems architectures for large, distributed, and dynamic urban environments.
