title: "Artificial Intelligence and Intellectual Debt"
description: Software engineers fall into intellectual debt when deploying ML-based systems without understanding how and why they work. For example, systems that integrate deep neural networks (DNNs) generate intellectual debt when these models misbehave and errors propagate through the whole system. In this talk, we will start giving context on the current AI narrative and the consequences of technocentrism. We will focus then on the intellectual debt problem and its implications for the software engineering lifecycle. We will explore how systems engineering principles can help us to address intellectual debt from a theoretical and practical perspective.
