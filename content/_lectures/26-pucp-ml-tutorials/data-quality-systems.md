## This course

- **AI for post-earthquake management.** Civil-engineering researchers in Lima. Online.
- This lead: **5, 12, 19 Oct** (data, linear models and perceptron, neural nets) and **23, 30 Nov** (language models).
- Other Mondays have other trainers (vision, physics-informed nets, agents).
- Slides in English. Talk and notebooks in Spanish.
- Today: **access** and **assess**. No model yet.

## USGS events, Peru, 2024

- Public CSV from the USGS earthquake catalog. The notebook downloads it.
- Window: 1 Jan 2024 through 1 Jan 2025, magnitude **4.5+**, latitudes **-18.5 to 0.2**, longitudes **-82 to -68**.
- Decision: which records are fit to use in a post-earthquake screening.
- Later sessions call the **same URL**. Do not change the dates.
