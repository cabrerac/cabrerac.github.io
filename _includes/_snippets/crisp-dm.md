## Data Preparation Depends on the Method

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-middle text-left" style="width: 100%">
                <p><b>More examples</b></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><b>k-means</b> - numbers on the same scale</li>
                    <li><b>Density clustering</b> - a distance between every two items</li>
                    <li><b>TF-IDF with a classifier</b> - a document by term matrix</li>
                    <li><b>Graph measures</b> - a list of links between items</li>
                    <li><b>Random forest</b> - a table with one row per item and one target</li>
                </ul>
                <p>There is no clean data in general. Data is ready for one method and one question.</p>
