## CRISP-DM

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">

## CRISP-DM

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-middle text-left" style="width: 50%">
                <p><b>Six phases</b></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><b>Business understanding:</b> what we need to decide</li>
                    <li><b>Data understanding:</b> what the data can say</li>
                    <li><b>Data preparation:</b> the data a method can use</li>
                    <li><b>Modeling:</b> the method runs</li>
                    <li><b>Evaluation:</b> is the answer good enough</li>
                    <li><b>Deployment:</b> the answer is used</li>
                </ul>
            </div>
