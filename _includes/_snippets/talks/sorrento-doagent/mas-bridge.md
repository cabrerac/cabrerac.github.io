                <p>In a multi-agent system, the intellectual debt <b>compounds</b>:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>Multiple agents making independent decisions</li>
                    <li>Interactions produce emergent behaviour</li>
                    <li>Coordination through implicit or explicit communication</li>
                    <li>Tracing <i>which</i> agent caused <i>which</i> outcome is hard</li>
                </ul>
                <br>
                <p><b>How can we design multi-agent systems where agent behaviour is observable, traceable, and interpretable?</b></p>
