## Self-Sustaining Software Systems (S4) — Problem and vision

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-middle text-left" style="width: 100%">
                <p>Software systems are pervasive and must respond to changing goals, dynamic data, unexpected failures, and security threats. Modern systems are so sophisticated that their complexity exceeds human comprehension, which limits interpretability and human control.</p>
                <p>Yet these systems must adapt autonomously as human intervention is not always feasible. This creates a tension between <b>autonomy</b> and <b>control</b> that current paradigms struggle to resolve.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
</div>

## S4 — From feedback loops to knowledge loops

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-middle text-left" style="width: 100%">
                <p>Current autonomous computing relies on <b>feedback loops with limited knowledge</b>, constraining both self-adaptation and explainability (e.g., MAPE-K architecture based on RL).</p>
                <p><b>S4</b> proposes replacing feedback loops with <b>knowledge loops</b> that integrate three sources of knowledge:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li><b>Design knowledge: </b> Developers encode data in human readable artefacts</li>
                    <li><b>Deployed system: </b> Data generated by the system during its lifecycle</li>
                    <li><b>Empirical knowledge: </b> Software engineering community expertise</li>
                </ul>
                <p>These knowledge loops act as <b>interfaces</b> that enable systems to self-analyse, self-maintain, self-adapt, and remain under human control.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
</div>

## S4 — Impact

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-middle text-left" style="width: 100%">
                <p>S4 enables the development of <b>safe and sustainable AI-based software systems</b> that remain under human control and steering.</p>
                <p>Outputs will benefit engineers, practitioners, and the general public through advances that different communities can use to build sustainable AI-based systems that self-adapt and remain under human control.</p>
                <p><em>Reference:</em> Cabrera, Paleyes, Lawrence. Self-sustaining Software Systems (S4): Towards Improved Interpretability and Adaptation. SATrends '24.</p>

## S4 — Milestones

                <ul>
                    <li><b>WP1</b> — Define knowledge sources and model their interfaces in software systems.</li>
                    <li><b>WP2</b> — Design knowledge-loop architectures that integrate design models, system data, and empirical software engineering knowledge.</li>
                    <li><b>WP3</b> — Build interpretable and adaptive mechanisms that exploit knowledge loops for autonomous decision-making.</li>
