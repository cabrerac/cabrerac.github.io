                    <li>The current AI narrative creates false promises and could cause harm. However, two basic scientific principles are an antidote we can all use: <b>critical thinking</b> and <b>openness</b>.</li>
                    <li>These principles help us identify problems such as <b>intellectual debt</b> and propose solutions such as <b>data-oriented architectures (DOAs)</b>.</li>
                    <li><b>DOAgent</b> implements DOA principles to mitigate intellectual debt in AI-based systems. It pushes toward observability, traceability, and explicit handling of policy factorisation in Multi-Agent Systems.</li>
                    <li>Current LLMs illustrate the <b>Consistent Reasoning Paradox</b>: they are trained to always answer, which makes them <b>difficult to trust</b> in the strong sense the paradox describes. The <b>Turing Test</b> points to a different behaviour in practice: <em>the ability to say "I don't know"</em> (or <em>"I don't understand"</em>).</li>
