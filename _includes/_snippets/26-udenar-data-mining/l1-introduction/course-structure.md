                   <ul>
                      <li><b>Course mark:</b> 60% formative (weeks 1 to 3) + 40% final project</li>
                      <li><b>Weekly formative (20% per week):</b>

                            <li>Group notebook due on Tuesdays and individual reflection due on Wednesdays</li>

                      </li>
                      <li><b>Final project (40% of the course):</b>
                         <ul>
                            <li>Group part, 70% of the project: the project notebook, the requirements, and the slides. The slides are a separate Moodle task and are not inside the zip.</li>
                            <li>Individual project reflection: 30% of the project</li>
                         </ul>
                      </li>
                      <li>Personal learning journal and Saturday reading discussions are required but not graded</li>
                   </ul>
