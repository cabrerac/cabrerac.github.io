## Course Structure

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-top text-left" style="width: 100%">
               <p><b>Assessment</b></p>
                  <ul>
                     <li><b>Course mark:</b> 60% formative (weeks 1 to 3) + 40% final project</li>
                     <li><b>Weekly formative (20% per week):</b>
                        <ul>
                           <li>Group notebook 12%, individual reflection PDF 8%</li>
                           <li>Group notebook due on Mondays and individual reflection due on Tuesdays</li>
                        </ul>
                     </li>
                     <li><b>Final project (40% of the course):</b>
                        <ul>
                           <li>Group deliverables: 70% of the project:
                              <ul>
                                 <li>Integrated notebook or source code</li>
                                 <li>Pipeline diagram</li>
                                 <li>Decision brief</li>
                                 <li>Live presentation</li>
                              </ul>
                           </li>
                           <li>Individual project reflection: 30% of the project</li>
                        </ul>
                     </li>
                     <li>Personal learning journal and Saturday reading discussions are required but not graded</li>
                  </ul>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
