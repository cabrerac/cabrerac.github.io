## The Data Dichotomy

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-middle text-left" style="width: 100%">
                <p><b>The Data Dichotomy</b>: data-driven systems must expose data, but service-oriented and object-oriented design hides it behind interfaces <a href="https://www.confluent.io/blog/data-dichotomy-rethinking-the-way-we-treat-data-and-services/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">(Stopford, 2016)</a>.</p>
                <p>This limits observability, adaptivity, and governance — and sits at the root of <b>intellectual debt</b> in deployed ML systems <a href="https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/cambridge-handbook-of-responsible-artificial-intelligence/intellectual-debt/F5D4CF05857D072ABED383AE7A3222E4" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">(Zittrain, 2022)</a>.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
</div>

## The Interfaces Research Agenda

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-middle text-left" style="width: 100%">
                <p>Two complementary projects address the dichotomy and mitigate intellectual debt:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li><b>DOCS — Data-Oriented Computing Systems:</b> data-oriented architectures (DOAs) that prioritise data over control flow.</li>
                    <li><b>S4 — Self-Sustaining Software Systems:</b> building blocks for autonomous systems that self-sustain while keeping humans in control.</li>
                </ul>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
</div>

## Programme Objectives

<div class="rows" style="height: 100%">
    <div class="row" style="height: 100%">
        <div class="columns" style="width: 100%">
            <div class="column vertical-middle text-left" style="width: 100%">
                <ul>
                    <li><b>Design, architect, and build</b> novel paradigms to design, develop, deploy, and decommission AI-based systems.</li>
                    <li><b>Interpret AI-based systems</b> with techniques that improve interpretability of autonomous behaviour while keeping humans steering.</li>
                </ul>
                <p>Validation domains include IoT, edge/local-first systems, healthcare, and security.</p>
