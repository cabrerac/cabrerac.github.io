## Prompt / RAG / FT

| Approach | Adds | Best when | Main risk |
|----------|------|-----------|-----------|
| Prompt-only | Instructions | Public knowledge and clear format | Brittleness. No private docs |
| RAG | External evidence | Answers must cite a corpus | Bad retrieval. Over-trust |
| Light FT | Weight updates | Stable specialised behaviour | Leakage, cost, drift |
