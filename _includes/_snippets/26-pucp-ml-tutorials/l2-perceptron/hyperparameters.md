                    <li><p><b>Epochs:</b> number of complete passes through the dataset</p></li>
                    <li><p><b>Batch size $m$:</b> training examples per mini-batch update</p></li>
                    <li><p><b>Regularisation strength $\lambda$:</b> weight of the complexity penalty in the cost function</p></li>
                    <li><p><b>Max iterations:</b> stopping limit for the perceptron learning algorithm</p></li>
